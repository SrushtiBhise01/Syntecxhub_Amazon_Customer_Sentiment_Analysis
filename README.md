
# Amazon Customer Sentiment Analysis 📊

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Amazon customer reviews to understand customer sentiment, product performance, satisfaction levels, and purchasing behavior. An interactive dashboard was developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to transform review data into meaningful insights that can support better business decisions.


## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze positive, negative, and neutral customer sentiment.
- Measure overall customer satisfaction through ratings.
- Compare customer sentiment across product categories.
- Understand the relationship between product ratings and sentiment scores.
- Compare sentiment among verified and non-verified purchases.
- Identify products receiving the highest number of negative reviews.
- Track monthly sentiment trends and customer engagement through helpful votes.

## 🛠️ Tools and Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI**
- **Power Query**
- **DAX**
- **Data Visualization**

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Customer Sentiment

Positive reviews account for 52.27% of the dataset, making them the largest sentiment group. However, negative reviews represent 28.42%, indicating opportunities to investigate customer complaints and improve product experiences.

### 2. Customer Satisfaction

The overall average rating is 3.47 out of 5. This indicates moderate customer satisfaction rather than overwhelmingly positive feedback.

### 3. Product Category Performance

Electronics has the highest review volume, with 738 reviews, followed by Home & Kitchen with 604 reviews.

Beauty has the lowest average category rating at approximately 3.1, while Electronics leads at approximately 3.7.

### 4. Rating and Sentiment Relationship

The rating distribution shows that reviews with ratings of 1–3 are predominantly negative, while five-star reviews are predominantly positive. Four-star reviews contain a mix of negative and positive sentiment classifications.

### 5. Verified Purchases

Verified purchases represent 81.79% of the reviews. The dashboard also enables comparison of sentiment distributions between verified and non-verified purchases.

### 6. Products Requiring Attention

The products with the highest negative-review counts include:

- Jacket — 190
- Lip Balm — 167
- Earbuds — 166
- Fitness Gloves — 165
- Novel — 160
- Non Stick Pan — 154
- Power Bank — 153

These products could be prioritized for further review analysis to identify recurring complaints and potential improvements.

### 7. Customer Engagement

The dataset records approximately 33K helpful votes, providing an additional measure of engagement with customer reviews.


## 🏁 Conclusion

This project demonstrates how customer review data can be transformed into an interactive analytical dashboard. By combining sentiment analysis, ratings, product categories, purchase verification, and negative-review counts, the dashboard helps highlight customer satisfaction patterns and areas requiring further investigation.
